The Problem

Associates feel disconnected — even in the same office

At WGT Bangalore, most associates interact only within their immediate team. New joiners take 3–6 months to feel truly settled. Cross-team friendships that drive innovation and retention rarely form organically.

  • ⏱️
    New joiners take too long to settle. Without structured social connection, the first 90 days are isolating — especially for associates relocating to Bangalore.
  • 📉
    Attrition peaks in year 1–2. Industry data shows 60%+ of early attrition is driven by poor belonging and lack of peer community, not compensation.
  • 🧱
    Teams stay in silos. Associates in Finance, GTO, and Supply Chain rarely interact outside their project. Cross-team energy that drives big ideas is missing.
  • 👨‍👩‍👧
    Working parents are underserved. Associates with kids have no structured way to connect with other WGT parents for school tips, carpooling, or family outings in Bangalore.
The Solution

SocialBling — verified associate circles on Slack

SocialBling is an interest-based community platform built for Walmart associates. Associates join circles by interest, and each circle gets a dedicated #social-bling-* Slack channel. Zero new tools — it lives where associates already work.

What's Already Built

This isn't a future idea — it's ready to pilot today

A working prototype of the WGT community hub is already live at socialbling.in/walmart-meetup. It includes all 9 circles, family meetups at Cubbon Park / Lalbagh / Bannerghatta, Slack channel integration, and a teams grid covering 18 WGT functions.

The platform is built on standard web technologies — no infrastructure cost, no new tool procurement required. Associates join circles and get routed directly to their #social-bling-* Slack channels.

The Pilot Plan

90 days · 50 volunteers · zero budget ask

1
Week 1–2

Recruit 50 volunteer associates

Spread across 5 teams. Mix of new joiners (<6 months) and established associates. Create the 9 Slack channels under #social-bling-* naming.

2
Week 3–8

Run circles & events

Each circle self-organizes: morning runs, lunch collectives, family outings (Cubbon Park, Lalbagh), WIT talks, wellness check-ins. Facilitator spends ~1hr/week total.

3
Week 10–12

Measure & report

Pulse survey at 30 and 90 days. Track: belonging score, new joiner confidence rating, cross-team connection count. Compare to non-pilot control group.

4
Day 90

Decision: roll out or learn

Present data to People team. If NPS > 70 and belonging scores improve → full WGT rollout. If not → clear learnings documented, no sunk cost.

Why Now

The right time to build this is before we need it

Return-to-office has increased face time, but not connection. Associates are physically present but socially isolated across teams. The infrastructure for community (Slack, people, shared spaces) already exists — it just needs a structure to activate it.

SocialBling provides that structure with zero cost and very low risk. If it works, we have a playbook for every Walmart office globally. If it doesn't, we learn what our associates actually need to feel connected — which is valuable either way.
